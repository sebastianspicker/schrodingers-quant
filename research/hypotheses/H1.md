# H1 — Channel-breakout trend following on BTC/EUR (4h)

**Status:** predeclared 2026-09-24, before any data in the evaluation periods was
inspected. Changes after this date require a new hypothesis ID (H2, …), not edits.

## Claim

A long-only 20-day channel breakout with a 10-day channel exit on BTC/EUR 4h
candles earns a positive return after Kraken-level costs on chronological
held-out data, with a materially smaller maximum drawdown than buy-and-hold.

The value sought is **drawdown-controlled participation in trends**, not beating
buy-and-hold in rising markets. Positive held-out results do not establish a
durable edge; they only permit forward paper testing.

## Rules (fixed; no tuning)

| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Market | Kraken spot BTC/EUR (live target). ETH/EUR is a robustness check only and is never selected on results. |
| Timeframe | 4h, closed candles only (`process_only_new_candles`). Decisions on candle close; fills at the next candle open in backtests. |
| Entry | Close > highest high of the previous 120 candles (20 days), excluding the current candle. Long only. |
| Exit | Close < lowest low of the previous 60 candles (10 days), excluding the current candle. |
| Catastrophe stop | Fixed −20 % from entry (exchange-hosted in a live profile). Not the primary exit. |
| ROI table | None. |
| Sizing | One position, fixed stake (the configured stake amount). No pyramiding, no leverage. |
| Protections | `CooldownPeriod` 1 candle; `StoplossGuard` 2 stop-outs in 30 days → 7-day lock; `MaxDrawdown` 25 % over 90 days → 30-day lock. |

The 20/10 channel lengths are the classic "Turtle System 1" values, chosen from
the literature, not fitted to this data.

## Data and periods

- Source: Binance BTC/EUR and ETH/EUR OHLCV used as a price proxy for Kraken
  (see [ADR-0002](../../docs/adr/0002-research-data-proxy.md)); proxy validity is
  checked on the overlapping recent Kraken candles.
- Train (sanity only, no parameter selection): 2020-01-01 → 2022-12-31.
- Validation: 2023-01-01 → 2024-06-30.
- Held-out, **run once** after the strategy is frozen: 2024-07-01 → 2026-09-20.
- Sensitivity (reported, never used to select): channel pairs 10/5 and 55/20 on
  train + validation only.

## Costs

Base case 0.5 % per side (Kraken base taker fee 0.40 % + 0.10 % slippage/spread
allowance). Stress case 1.0 % per side. The actual account fee tier is checked by
the live preflight (Slice C).

## Metrics reported

Net total return, CAGR, max drawdown (equity), return/max-drawdown, trade count,
win rate, average holding time, exposure (share of time in a position); the same
return and drawdown for buy-and-hold and 0 for cash, over identical periods.

## Decision rule (evaluated on BTC/EUR)

- **K1** Held-out net return (base costs) > 0.
- **K2** Held-out max drawdown ≤ 0.6 × buy-and-hold max drawdown, same period.
- **K3** Held-out net return > 0 under stress costs.
- **K4** Validation net return (base costs) > 0.
- **GO** if K1–K4 all pass. GO permits forward paper trading and the €10
  execution pilot; it does not justify adding capital.
- If the held-out period has fewer than 5 closed trades, the result is
  **INCONCLUSIVE**: record it and treat as GO for forward paper testing only.
- Otherwise **NO-GO**: the strategy stays out of dry-run defaults and H2 is
  written.

## Known limits

- The author (an AI model) has general knowledge of crypto price history up to
  mid-2026. Choosing a trend-following family may be influenced by that
  knowledge even though parameters come from the literature. Forward paper
  results are therefore required before any capital decision.
- A few trades per year means wide uncertainty on any held-out statistic.
- Binance proxy prices differ from Kraken fills; fills at the next candle open
  ignore order-book depth (irrelevant at €8, relevant later).
