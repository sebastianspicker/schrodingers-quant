## In short

- **What is tested.** Whether a simple trend-following rule on BTC/EUR makes
  money after realistic trading costs on data it was never tuned on, while
  losing clearly less at its worst point than simply buying and holding.
- **How it is judged.** Four pass criteria (K1–K4), fixed here before the test
  data was looked at. All four must pass.
- **What a pass allows.** Only forward paper trading and a €10 live execution
  test. A pass does not show a lasting edge and does not justify adding capital.

## Claim

A long-only 20-day channel breakout with a 10-day channel exit on BTC/EUR 4h
candles earns a positive return after Kraken-level costs on chronological
held-out data, with a materially smaller maximum drawdown than buy-and-hold.

In plain terms: buy when the price closes above its highest point of the last
20 days, sell when it closes below its lowest point of the last 10 days, and
test this on a later stretch of data that was kept aside.

The value sought is **drawdown-controlled participation in trends**, not beating
buy-and-hold in rising markets. Positive held-out results do not establish a
durable edge; they only permit forward paper testing.

## Rules (fixed; no tuning)

| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Market | Kraken spot BTC/EUR (live target). ETH/EUR is a robustness check only and is never selected on results. |
| Timeframe | 4h, closed candles only (`process_only_new_candles`). Decisions on candle close; fills at the next candle open in backtests. |
| Entry | Close > highest high of the previous 120 candles (20 days), excluding the current candle. Long only. |
| Exit | Close < lowest low of the previous 60 candles (10 days), excluding the current candle. |
| Catastrophe stop | Fixed −20 % from entry (exchange-hosted in a live profile). Not the primary exit. |
| ROI table | None. |
| Sizing | One position, fixed stake (the configured stake amount). No pyramiding, no leverage. |
| Protections | `CooldownPeriod` 1 candle; `StoplossGuard` 2 stop-outs in 30 days → 7-day lock; `MaxDrawdown` 25 % over 90 days → 30-day lock. |

The 20/10 channel lengths are the classic "Turtle System 1" values, chosen from
the literature, not fitted to this data.

What the protections do, in plain terms: after any exit the bot waits one
candle; two stop-outs within 30 days pause new entries for 7 days; a 25 %
drawdown within 90 days pauses new entries for 30 days.

## Data and periods

- Source: Binance BTC/EUR and ETH/EUR OHLCV (open, high, low, close, volume)
  used as a price proxy for Kraken
  (see [ADR-0002](../../docs/adr/0002-research-data-proxy.md)); proxy validity is
  checked on the overlapping recent Kraken candles.
- Train (sanity only, no parameter selection): 2020-01-01 → 2022-12-31.
- Validation: 2023-01-01 → 2024-06-30.
- Held-out, **run once** after the strategy is frozen: 2024-07-01 → 2026-09-20.
- Sensitivity (reported, never used to select): channel pairs 10/5 and 55/20 on
  train + validation only.

## Costs

Base case 0.5 % per side: Kraken's base taker fee of 0.40 % plus a 0.10 %
allowance for slippage and spread. Stress case 1.0 % per side. "Per side" means
the cost is paid on the buy and again on the sell. The actual account fee tier
is checked by the live preflight (Slice C).

## Metrics reported

For H1: net total return, CAGR (compound annual growth rate), max drawdown
(equity), return divided by max drawdown, trade count, win rate, average
holding time, and exposure (share of time in a position). For comparison, the
same return and drawdown for buy-and-hold, and 0 for cash, over identical
periods.

## Decision rule (evaluated on BTC/EUR)

- **K1** Held-out net return (base costs) > 0.
- **K2** Held-out max drawdown ≤ 0.6 × buy-and-hold max drawdown, same period.
- **K3** Held-out net return > 0 under stress costs.
- **K4** Validation net return (base costs) > 0.
- **GO** if K1–K4 all pass. GO permits forward paper trading and the €10
  execution pilot; it does not justify adding capital.
- If the held-out period has fewer than 5 closed trades, the result is
  **INCONCLUSIVE**: record it and treat as GO for forward paper testing only.
- Otherwise **NO-GO**: the strategy stays out of dry-run defaults and H2 is
  written.

K2 in plain terms: if buy-and-hold's worst fall over the held-out period is,
say, 50 %, H1's worst fall must be 30 % or less.

## Known limits

- The author (an AI model) has general knowledge of crypto price history up to
  mid-2026. Choosing a trend-following family may be influenced by that
  knowledge even though parameters come from the literature. Forward paper
  results are therefore required before any capital decision.
- A few trades per year means wide uncertainty on any held-out statistic.
- Binance proxy prices differ from Kraken fills; fills at the next candle open
  ignore order-book depth (irrelevant at €8, relevant later).

## Glossary

| Term | Meaning here |
| --- | --- |
| Channel breakout | Entering when the price breaks above the range (channel) of recent highs. |
| 4h candle | One four-hour bar of open, high, low and close prices. A closed candle is one whose four hours are over. |
| Long only | The strategy only buys and later sells; it never bets on falling prices. |
| Held-out data | Data kept aside and used once, after the rules are frozen, to test them. |
| Train / validation | Earlier periods: train is a sanity check only; validation is a second check before the held-out run. |
| Drawdown | The fall from the highest equity reached so far to a later low, as a percentage. |
| Buy-and-hold | Buying BTC/EUR at the start of a period and holding it to the end, with the same costs. |
| Catastrophe stop | A last-resort exit at −20 % from the entry price. |
| ROI table | Freqtrade's optional take-profit schedule; H1 uses none. |
| Pyramiding | Adding to a position that is already open; H1 does not. |
| Slippage | The difference between the expected price and the price actually filled. |
| Taker fee | The exchange fee for an order that fills immediately against the order book. |
| Proxy prices | Binance prices used in place of Kraken's for the backtests; see ADR-0002. |
| Forward paper trading | Dry-run trading on data that did not exist when the rules were fixed. |
| Slice C | A work-package name from the project's original plan. From context it means the live tooling, whose preflight is now `sq.live.preflight` (inferred, not stated in the original). |
